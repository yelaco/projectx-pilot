Trading schedule

One recurring window per trading weekday, in America/Chicago. This manager supplies the schedule to every assigned automation; instrument names do not select a calendar.

Default: 17:00 → 15:00 last entry → 15:05 exit reminder. The exit reminder reports attention and never closes a position. Choose a narrower window for products with earlier closes or daily pauses; this schedule does not establish product availability. Reviewed 2026 holiday cutoffs can only shorten it. Unknown holiday years remain blocked. General/manager operation days and the news filter also apply. Changing this manager pauses its profiles; confirm their updated trading setup before restarting. Existing trades keep their captured closing window.
